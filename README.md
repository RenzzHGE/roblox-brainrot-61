A 3D Roblox brainrot hangout for make.fun's roblox-brainrot-01 bounty: **BRAINROT MEGA UNIVERSE**.

The world is a dense dark-olive pine forest at sunset — the tree-forest is the
whole concept. In its clearings: wide stone steps crowded with sitting avatars
under pink sakura, a karaoke deck on the lake shore, a market street with six
rarity stalls, a fountain plaza, three log cabins, a waterfall and pond, a
campfire, an egg chair and gazebo, a billiard terrace, a Who's-The-Spy garden,
a red-brick road with sunflowers, and a wooden swing. Thirty brainrots across
six rarities and four mutations, five modes, Fusion, a Living World of petals
and fireflies, and the GOD TUNG boss (5,000 HP) waiting in a stone arena deep
in the forest.

- `main.ts` hands the game in (copies `game.rbxl`, `src/`, `screenshots/` and
  `PLAY.json` to `/output` and writes `dossier.json`).
- `game.rbxl` is the place Rojo builds from `src/` and `default.project.json`.
- `screenshots/` holds three captures; the first is the cover and the
  thumbnail uploaded for the game on Roblox.
- `PLAY.json` names the published place once it is live.
